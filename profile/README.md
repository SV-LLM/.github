# SV-LLM

SV-LLM is the StegVerse organization for coordinated, attributable intelligence work. Participating LLMs and AI entities provide candidate intelligence; they do not acquire StegVerse governance authority by participating.

## Organization

```text
SV-LLM/
├── .github/                    shared organization architecture and contracts
├── OpenAI/                     OpenAI capability surface
├── Sandbox/                    multi-entity collaboration plane
├── StegVerse-AI-Entity/       ecosystem orchestration/evidence-matching entity
└── <Additional-LLM>/           additional provider capability repos
```

### Roles

- **.github** — canonical SV-LLM organization structure, shared contracts, work-context/Sandbox interfaces, authority boundaries and documentation.
- **OpenAI** — OpenAI-specific intelligence capabilities and attributable contribution envelopes; no governance authority.
- **Sandbox** — provider-neutral multi-entity work sessions, disagreement/refusal retention, synthesis provenance and governance handoff packaging.
- **StegVerse-AI-Entity** — reconstructs ecosystem purpose, matches evidence against receipted history, recognizes capability gaps and orchestrates appropriate intelligence work; it is not defined as an LLM.
- **Additional LLM repositories** — provider/entity-specific capability declarations and interfaces following the same non-authority contract.

## Governing principle

Knowledge, inference, governance and execution remain distinct. Ecosystem AI matches manifested claims/proposed transitions against admissible evidence and reconstructable receipted history. The Inference Window separately evaluates what might or might not become reachable under every applicable Admissibility Matrix disposition.

Canonical ecosystem truth remains in its authoritative StegVerse repositories and is resolved through InTr as relevant context; provider repositories do not duplicate it.
